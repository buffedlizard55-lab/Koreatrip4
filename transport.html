Public transport, without invented timings

Use the official route as the backbone.

These rows begin at planned hotels or city anchors where an authority/operator source supports them. They do not turn approximate map estimates into fixed journey times, and they keep missing flight and KTX details visible.

Before choosing a route. Confirm the travel year, flight arrival terminal/time and KTX station pair. Then use current operator schedules and a live public-transport planner. Fares, service patterns, queues and weather conditions change.

Transit notes

Practical routing cues

Airport to Myeongdong

Stanford’s own FAQ names Limousine 6015 and the Ibis Myeongdong alighting point. AREX can be a useful alternative to Seoul Station, but it does not itself complete the hotel transfer. Pick after terminal and baggage timing are known.

Namsan access

For the tower, the official operator documents the Myeongdong Station Exit 5 cable-car approach and Namsan Circular 01A/01B. The circular buses require cashless/card payment; no fixed hotel-to-tower duration is asserted here.

Busan city movement

Busan’s public guide supports the Line 1 → Seomyeon → Line 2 structure from Busan Station to Haeundae. It also anchors the beach at Haeundae Station exits 3/5. Use live routing for platform, exit and final hotel walk.

Temple day

Visit Busan publishes the Haeundae Station Exit 7 bus options for Haedong Yonggungsa. Build the return around the live bus timetable and the temple’s stated 18:50 last entry — especially in shorter daylight months.
