Decisions before booking

Nothing uncertain is hidden.

These items do not invalidate the trip. They identify what needs a traveler decision, a current branch check or a live schedule before the itinerary can be treated as a timed plan.

Prioritization. Resolve high-priority items first: dates, flight/terminal, KTX stations/timing and the open final days. Then refine venue branches and merchant-specific plans. A red issue is a planning dependency — not a judgment on the venue.

Recommended order

A short path from draft to bookable

1. Lock the calendar

Confirm the actual year and all dates. The current weekday/year conflict must be resolved before interpreting weekday-only hours, rail inventory or attraction closures.

2. Add arrival and rail details

Record flight arrival terminal/time, then select the airport route. Enter the actual KTX station pair/date in KORAIL before connecting the rail day to the Busan hotel plan.

3. Turn names into exact pins

For each must-do restaurant, shop or activity, capture an exact branch address and current official operating page. Keep flexible alternatives as flexible.

4. Fill departure day last

Once a flight or rail departure is known, add the transfer and luggage buffer to Day 8. Until then, an open day is more honest and more useful.
