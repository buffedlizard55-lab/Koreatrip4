Build each day around the anchors

Seoul base: Myeongdong

The Stanford hotel source provides its own airport-bus guidance and stated check-in/out timing. That makes it a reliable trip anchor, while generic Myeongdong retail hours remain store-specific.

Palace morning

Gyeongbokgung’s winter closing time and the guard ceremony provide a defensible schedule framework. Build a buffer for the palace’s final admission and any official closure notice rather than packing in an unnamed meal stop.

Seongsu to COEX

Several Seongsu stops have confirmed address/hours, but they should be sequenced using a live map on the day. COEX’s direct Samseong Station connection is more robust than an unsourced cross-city duration.

Busan base: Haeundae

Haeundae Station and the beach are official transit/location anchors. The temple and Jagalchi are viable day elements, but their bus/metro legs should be timed through current service rather than a fixed template.
