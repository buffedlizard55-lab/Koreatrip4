Arrival terminal/time is absent, while the KTX leg has no selected stations or train. Keep dinner and hotel-transfer timing flexible until the actual flight and rail booking are known.

Crystal Jade, several Seongsu fashion stops, and multiple restaurant names are retained but not routed as fixed points. A current operator-confirmed address is needed before they become navigable stops.
