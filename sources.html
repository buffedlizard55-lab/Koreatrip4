Dashboard / Sources

Reviewable research

Open the evidence yourself.

The register links to official operators, public authorities, tourism authorities and business-owned pages used in this dashboard. The summary tells you exactly what was carried into the tables — no source is used to support more than its accessible content showed.

Source method. The strongest evidence is a current operator or public-authority page. A social profile can establish business identity but is not treated as confirmation of current hours unless it states them clearly. All linked pages should be reconfirmed before travel.

Scope & limitations

What the research does not do

No inferred values

The dashboard does not derive a flight terminal, a KTX station, a generic store branch, a live travel time, a USD conversion or an opening hour from an unrelated source.

Operator changes happen

Transit operators can suspend services and hotels, attractions, markets and shops can adjust hours, admissions, holidays and tenant lists. The source links are a handoff for the final pre-travel check.
